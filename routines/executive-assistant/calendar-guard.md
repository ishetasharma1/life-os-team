# Routine: Calendar Guard (daily 06:41)

Agent: Executive Assistant. Read `CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/agents/executive-assistant.md` first. Sends NO message: the 07:10 Morning Brief reports the results.

1. **Read** today's and tomorrow's events on her own calendars (Google Calendar).
2. **Protect the rhythm:** 05:00 ritual, 06:00 training, 09:00–12:00 Career Power Morning, AI learning, dog walks, meal prep, reviews. If something new clashes with these, move or shorten HER OWN flexible block (never the appointment created by someone else) and log it.
3. **Buffers and travel time:** for appointments away from home, add a travel block before and after (estimate from the address in the event; if none, 30 minutes) unless one exists.
4. **Prep packs:** for each call or appointment today and tomorrow: who, why, last contact (search Gmail), documents to bring, 3 questions to ask.
5. **Placing items:** time blocks for anything she has booked (from Outbox items or confirmation emails) that is not yet in the calendar.
6. **Outbox:** one item "CALENDAR — [date]" (Priority Today) with prep packs and every change (event, before → after, why).

Rules: never delete an event; never touch events created by others; never send, accept or decline invitations. If a clash involves someone else's event, flag it only. Finish with one Run Log row (Routine = calendar-guard) listing every change.
