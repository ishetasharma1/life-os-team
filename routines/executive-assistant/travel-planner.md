Agent: Executive Assistant. Read `CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/agents/executive-assistant.md` first. Outputs go to a Notion sub-page of the "9. ## Every trip
- Options and timings (Booking.com search for stays; web search for ferries, flights, trains). Never book, pay or enter her details.
- Documents: passport validity (expiry date only), EES/ETIAS rules for the dates, insurance, pet passports.
- Calendar blocks for travel days, with buffers.
- Pet cover: Luna, Louie, Savi travel with her or Karen covers; check with the Vet Agent's Outbox for any pet travel requirements.

## Reading ↔ Maidbronn checklist
- 2 weeks: route chosen (ferry or flight); pet travel plan or Karen booked; anything to take to Germany listed.
- 1 week: post and parcels paused or redirected; bins schedule both ends; Maidbronn heating and utilities ready.
- Day before: Reading house closed (heating, windows, plugs, alarm, plants); car checked; pet bag packed.
- Return: Maidbronn house closed the same way; Reading house reopened.

## Holiday checklist
- 6 weeks: travel insurance, documents, pet cover booked, out-of-office dates in the calendar.
- 2 weeks: currency/cards, transfers, activities, packing list.
- 1 week: out-of-office drafted (Gmail draft), check-in dates, home prep.
- Day before: check-in, boarding passes, chargers, medication, pet handover notes.

Each checklist stage appears on the deadline radar on its due date. Finish with one Run Log row (Routine = travel-planner).
