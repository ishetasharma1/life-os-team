# Routine: Week Ahead (Sundays 17:13)

Agent: Chief of Staff. Read `CLAUDE.md` and `.claude/agents/chief-of-staff.md` first.

ONE message that sets up her week. It also serves as the Sunday brief.

1. **The week at a glance:** Monday–Sunday calendar: key events, appointments, travel and social plans (skip routine blocks). Flag clashes, back-to-back days and days with no breathing room.
2. **This week's Big 3:** take them from today's Weekly CEO Review page in Reviews & Coaching (written by the Life Coach this morning). Don't invent new ones.
3. **Deadline radar:** everything due in the next 14 days, with dates.
4. **Calls to make this week:** one list grouped Family · Health & pets (GP, vet, dentist) · Admin & insurers · Money · Other, from Life Maintenance (due/overdue items that need a call), the waiting-for tracker, open tasks and recent emails. Include phone numbers or links only if they appear in her own emails or Notion.
5. **Waiting-for and owed-by-me:** the full current lists.
6. **Urgent email:** any 🔴 since Saturday morning, with drafts.
7. **Agent Outbox:** all Queued "This week" items; mark them "Included in message".
8. Last line: accountant-sheet nudge for the weekend's transactions.

Keep it scannable: one table for the week, short lists for the rest. Safety as in the morning brief. Finish with one Agent Run Log row (Routine = week-ahead).
